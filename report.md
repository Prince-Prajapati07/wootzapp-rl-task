# MiniShop RL Report

All numbers below are computed from JSONL logs generated by `run.sh`.

## 1. Success Rate

- Random: 9/200 = 0.045, 95% Wilson CI [0.024, 0.083]
- Q-learning: 168/200 = 0.840, 95% Wilson CI [0.783, 0.884]

## 2. Learning Curve

See `analysis/learning_curve.png`. It plots the mean of the three training runs with one standard deviation shading.

## 3. Popup Ablation

- popup_p=0.0: 168/200 = 0.840, CI [0.783, 0.884]
- popup_p=0.15: 168/200 = 0.840, CI [0.783, 0.884]
- popup_p=0.4: 168/200 = 0.840, CI [0.783, 0.884]

Success was identical at popup_p=0, 0.15, and 0.4: 168/200 in each case. On these seeds, the learned policy absorbed popup Dismiss clicks within the 20-step budget, so no episode outcome flipped.

## 4. Top Failure Reasons

- hit 20-step limit on product/quantity screen: 32; example episode 9 goal green-cap x3 action click(0) screen product

## 5. Step Time

- Median step time: 29 ms
- 95th percentile step time: 105 ms

The dominant cost is CDP round trips plus re-observing the page after each action. Real mouse clicks add two CDP calls; wait actions mostly spend their fixed sleep.

## 6. Surprise

The random baseline sometimes does better than expected because the task is short and the visible button labels constrain bad choices. The Newsletter detour and popups are still enough to make it unreliable.
