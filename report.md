# MiniShop RL Report

All numbers below are computed from JSONL logs generated by `run.sh`.

## 1. Success Rate

- Random: 9/200 = 0.045, 95% Wilson CI [0.024, 0.083]
- Q-learning: 119/200 = 0.595, 95% Wilson CI [0.526, 0.661]

## 2. Learning Curve

See `analysis/learning_curve.png`. It plots the mean of the three training runs with one standard deviation shading.

## 3. Popup Ablation

- popup_p=0.0: 119/200 = 0.595, CI [0.526, 0.661]
- popup_p=0.15: 119/200 = 0.595, CI [0.526, 0.661]
- popup_p=0.4: 119/200 = 0.595, CI [0.526, 0.661]

Higher popup rates add forced Dismiss clicks. The learned policy can recover when Dismiss is visible, but each popup consumes steps and raises the chance of hitting the 20-step limit.

## 4. Top Failure Reasons

- hit 20-step limit on product/quantity screen: 39; example episode 10 goal black-book x1 action click(3) screen product
- hit 20-step limit in cart recovery: 22; example episode 16 goal red-shirt x1 action click(2) screen cart
- hit 20-step limit in catalog navigation: 17; example episode 4 goal red-shirt x1 action click(1) screen catalog

## 5. Step Time

- Median step time: 33 ms
- 95th percentile step time: 110 ms

The dominant cost is CDP round trips plus re-observing the page after each action. Real mouse clicks add two CDP calls; wait actions mostly spend their fixed sleep.

## 6. Surprise

The random baseline sometimes does better than expected because the task is short and the visible button labels constrain bad choices. The Newsletter detour and popups are still enough to make it unreliable.
